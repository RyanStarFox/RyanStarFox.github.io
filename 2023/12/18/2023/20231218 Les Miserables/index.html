# 法大悲 repo

有幸抢到了法大悲的票

于是在12月15日的交大金课工程学导论课下课后翘了晚上18:30的数学分析习题课前去上海文化广场观看

乘地铁紫竹高新区–>上海南站–>陕西南路

坐一号线的时候还有一个地铁迷兴奋地跑过来拍照，说这部地铁是上海一号线第一辆列车

感觉很好运

因为好久没有听音乐剧了，打算在路上重听一下10周年版的大悲，然后发现自己的耳朵进化了，已经听得出AirPods 2无法承受这种宏大的交响乐了，在地铁上后悔没有把大馒头4带出来。

在上海南站换乘的时候听到《The Confrontation》已经听得头皮发麻了，非常期待！！

从陕西南路出来发现是环贸iapm，高考后莫名其妙对这里非常熟，打卡圣诞Apple store

晚饭在一家深井陈记烤鹅吃了雪菜牛肉饭，一直很喜欢吃这道菜，很鲜。

没有给饭拍照（哎这次就是没想到应当多拍点照片）因为外面打光很好，想给自己拍张照

发现反光不够好

走到上海文化广场，路边的旗，，

进去了，人很多，见到一个女孩子拿着徕卡相机（不是镜头，是镜头+相机），感觉她很富有

看到上海电视台也在有那里采访，趁没有人偷偷拍了一张大海报（的一部分

买了一些纪念品：纪念册和CD

可以给大家看看纪念册里面，很精美！sukisuki

在进场的时候还偶遇xys小姐，小小震撼，，和她大眼瞪小眼

记得第一次看10周年的时候还有点无聊，因为确实10周年仅仅是一个音乐会，表演的部分不是很多，自己还没有定力只听音乐不做别的事情，眼睛没事情做。这一次看现场还是完全不懂的法语，隐隐约约有点担心自己会不会感觉无聊，，

但其实当《Prologue》的旋律一开始的时候，我就知道不可能会感到无聊，现场的声音和耳机里的声音是不好比的，每一个音符使灵魂震颤，’Pitié,pitié’一开口，我一下子很恍惚，真的不敢相信自己能在现场听到

我全程都很亢奋，尤其是《The Confrontation》、《Do you hear the people sing》、《One day more》

《Master of the house》很欢乐，《Beggars at the feast》大家真的在拍手

小珂赛特和伽弗洛什都很可爱，，喜欢《橱窗里的娃娃》，甜甜的小女孩谁不爱

冉阿让说“你命令我不要死，我会努力尝试”的时候真的热烈盈眶

不过也有一些缺点，忘记哪一段高潮的时候设备爆音了，一下子出戏了

第二幕的时候很多地方不该拍手，但是观众在乱拍手，导致几首歌之间的衔接被打断了

不过总体来说，真的远胜录像版本，毕竟大悲所有的版本里面，现场版是最好的！

# Les Misérables (French production) review

I was lucky enough to snag tickets for Les Misérables.

So after the Introduction to Engineering class at Jiao Tong University on December 15th, I skipped my Mathematical Analysis exercise class at 18:30 and went to Shanghai Culture Square to watch the show.

I took the subway from Zizhu High-tech Zone to Shanghai South Station, then to Shaanxi South Road.

While riding the Line 1, a fellow subway enthusiast excitedly came over to take photos, saying this train was the first one on Shanghai’s Line 1.

I felt really lucky.

Since it had been a while since I last watched a musical, I planned to re-listen to the 10th-anniversary version of Les Misérables on the way. I then realized my ears had evolved—I could tell that AirPods 2 couldn’t handle such grand symphonic music. I regretted not bringing my Big Muffin 4 with me on the subway.

When I heard The Confrontation while transferring at Shanghai South Station, I was already thrilled; I couldn’t wait to see the show!!

Upon exiting at Shaanxi South Road, I found myself at IAPM mall. I somehow became very familiar with this place after the college entrance exam, so I stopped by the Christmas-themed Apple Store to check it out.

For dinner, I had shredded vegetable and beef rice at Shenjing Chenji Roast Goose. I’ve always loved this dish—it’s incredibly savory.

I didn’t take a photo of the meal (sigh, I didn’t think to take more pictures this time) because the lighting outside was great, and I wanted to take a selfie.

I noticed the reflection wasn’t ideal.

Walking to Shanghai Culture Square, I saw the flags by the roadside:

I went inside—there were so many people! I saw a girl holding a Leica camera (not just the lens, but the whole camera set). She looked very wealthy.

I also saw Shanghai TV doing an interview there. While no one was looking, I secretly took a photo of part of the large poster:

I bought some souvenirs: a commemorative album and a CD.

Let me show you what’s inside the commemorative album—it’s really exquisite! sukisuki

When entering the venue, I even ran into Miss XYS—what a surprise! We just stared at each other.

I remember I found the 10th-anniversary version a little boring back then, because it was essentially just a concert with few performance segments. I lacked the discipline to focus solely on the music with nothing else to do, and my eyes had nothing to occupy them. This time, watching the live show with zero knowledge of French, I was vaguely worried I might get bored…

But as soon as the melody of Prologue started, I knew I wouldn’t be bored at all. The sound in person is incomparable to that from headphones—every note made my soul tremble. The moment ‘Pitié, pitié’ was sung, I was completely stunned; I couldn’t believe I was hearing it live.

I was excited throughout the entire show, especially during The Confrontation, Do you hear the people sing, and One day more.

Master of the house was so joyful, and everyone really clapped along during Beggars at the feast.

Little Cosette and Gavroche were so adorable; who wouldn’t love the sweet little girl in The Doll in the Window?

When Jean Valjean said, “You order me not to die, and I will try my best,” I was moved to tears.

There were a few flaws though: I forgot which climax had audio distortion, which broke the immersion for a moment.

During Act 2, there were many parts where clapping wasn’t appropriate, but the audience clapped randomly, disrupting the transitions between songs.

Overall, though, the live version is far superior to recorded versions. After all, among all versions of Les Misérables, the live version is the best!

I recorded two encores:

They sang Finale twice. During the second rendition, it felt like the actors wanted the audience to sing along, but unfortunately, no one knew French.

It’s a shame they didn’t sing Do you hear the people sing; I wonder if it was due to political reasons. That song is perfect for a chorus!

Curtain call!

A dreamlike evening.

On the way back, I was still buzzing with excitement and wanted to rewatch the 10th and 25th-anniversary movies and read the book. So I downloaded the e-book on the subway, put away The Catcher in the Rye, and started reading Les Misérables.

I still had to use AirPods Pro 2 to listen to Les Misérables on the subway—it was frustrating. As soon as I got back to my dorm, I switched to using Big Muffin. Big Muffin 4 is truly amazing! The soundstage advantage of over-ear headphones really shines when listening to symphonic music like this.

I definitely want to watch it again.

Sigh, it’s a pity I didn’t take many photos this time; I haven’t developed the habit of recording life through images anytime, anywhere.
