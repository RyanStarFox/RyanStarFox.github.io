20s啊！多少操场人已经排在我们前面了！

真是贴心呢，能不能现在就好

but

My experience with the iPhone launch sale

September 15, 2023, 8 PM: The thrilling iPhone launch rush

Mainland China participants: Leo, Bill, Ryan

Hong Kong region participant: Hertz

Let me first give you some background:

Leo: The richest guy among us, who switched from an iPhone 13 to an iPhone 15 Pro (from 2021 to 2023)

Ryan: Switched from an iPhone X to an iPhone 15 Pro (from 2018 to 2023)

Hertz: The most hardcore user, upgraded from an iPhone 6s to an iPhone 15 Pro (from 2017 to 2023)

Bill: The most business-savvy one; he helped me and Leo grab iPhones Pro, then sold the extra units on Xianyu.

At 7:48 PM, Hertz was already fully prepared and eager to go. Cook told him, “We’re just as impatient (to make money) as you are.”

I tried to calm Hertz down:

At 7:57 PM, the two of us started counting down:

Ten seconds left 🌭️, and we both stopped at the 10-second mark.

Then came 20 seconds of silence.

Twenty seconds! So many people had already queued up ahead of us!

Just when we were utterly despairing, Cook comforted us:

How thoughtful! Could it happen right now?

At 10:03 PM:

But

Ugh, even more hopeless. The Apple Store is just like a course registration platform.

Suddenly,

Except for Leo, everyone succeeded.

Leo was super 🌭️. I picked up the phone, and he told me he had too much money to spend.

His bank card had a spending limit.

All hope was lost.

So he ended up begging others for help.

How 🤡.

In the end, he also succeeded.

We all got orders, but the estimated delivery time never came.

Hertz was the first to get an estimated arrival time: the first batch on September 22!! He was the first to succeed.

But no news came from others.

In the middle of the night, while I was writing yesterday’s draft:

Suddenly, there was a “ding” in my earphones.

“I got one!” It startled my roommate who was playing Genshin Impact.

The results: Four of us grabbed 5 iPhones, all successfully! All were part of the September 22 launch batch!

One iPhone 15 Pro Max and three iPhone 15 Pro models, all with 8+256GB storage.

Leo:

Since Bill sent the two iPhones 15 Pro he grabbed to me for resale, I’ll receive 3 iPhone 15 Pro units to resell, and thus become an Apple dealer at Jiaotong University.

Also:

If we can make a profit from reselling on Xianyu, we’ll try again next year.

End //
