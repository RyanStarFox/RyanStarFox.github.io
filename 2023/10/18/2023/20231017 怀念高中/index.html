怀念高中

今天看到朋友圈里很多团委后两届小朋友发的部门合照，一下子很感慨，也很怀念SHS。

说来好笑，高三的时候大家都口口声声说盼望高三毕业，但是读大学读了一个月出头，朋友圈不知为何却刮起了“想回高三”的风潮。想回高三，这又未尝不是一种斯德哥尔摩呢？高三很苦，但是苦中作乐显得尤其的珍贵。我永远怀念那几个逃课的夜晚，在中兴楼的万圣节之夜，在四楼楼梯间和一楼门厅外柱子后面怯生生地长（发）聊（疯），团代会后在教工之家喝着免费的热可可和热巧克力狂欢（发疯*2）。感觉高三给我的感觉就是大家都是集中精力在冲高考，别的方面已经毫无顾忌了：食堂不好吃就天天出去吃，天天在教室里“热狗”“小丑”地嘴炮。可以肆意地发泄自己的不满情绪而不会有人介意。

高三的印记也有一些留到了大学。对我而言，最重要的印记是音乐。高三听了很多作业钢琴曲和睡眠钢琴曲，而我现在还保留着学习时听钢琴曲的习惯。听到那些熟悉的旋律，恍惚间似乎真的回到了那些大家都很累，有时集中精神有时在“自我放松”的晚自修。又一件说来好笑的事，我尝尝可以把在追的《星球大战》系列与高三联系起来，因为高三后期意外地发现星战的几首main theme虽然有些吵，但是能够帮助我集中注意力。打开Apple Music给我的推歌，本来想听点新的流行音乐的，结果全都是新的钢琴曲。感慨。有时候，记忆浮现需要一个契机，对我来说，其实最好的契机反而是嗅觉和听觉，没有视觉那样直白，但更加扣人心弦。

现在常常回SHS，几乎是每周回一次，去看望czy小姐，和她一起吃顿饭。每次回去我都有一种错觉，总觉得我是在和高三的自己重逢，就像电影里那种转头看见年轻的自己的镜头一样。我很感谢czy小姐在我高三时给我的陪伴，让我的高三生活不至于那样两点一线，给我带来了更多活力。现在，我想陪czy小姐一起度过她的高三，我说实话也并不确定这样经常去看她会不会影响她的学习，但我真的很希望她好，不仅仅是学业上，希望她在高三不要那样苦，依旧开心可爱，也希望我们最终能走到一起。

Nostalgia for High School

Today I saw group photos of the past two batches of Youth League committee members posted by my WeChat friends, which instantly filled me with emotion and made me miss SHS deeply.

It’s funny to think about it: in senior year of high school, everyone kept saying they couldn’t wait to graduate, but after starting university for just over a month, a trend of “wanting to go back to senior year” suddenly took off on WeChat Moments. Wanting to return to senior year—could this be a form of Stockholm syndrome? Senior year was tough, but finding joy in hardship made those moments especially precious. I will always cherish those nights we skipped class, the Halloween party at Zhongxing Building, the shy long chats we had in the stairwell on the fourth floor and behind the pillars outside the first-floor lobby, and the wild celebrations (two rounds of madness) drinking free hot cocoa and hot chocolate at the staff lounge after the Youth League congress. What I remember most about senior year is how everyone focused solely on preparing for the college entrance exam, with no worries about anything else: if the cafeteria food wasn’t good, we’d eat out every day, and we’d spend all our time in class arguing about random topics like “hot dog” and “clown”. We could freely vent our frustrations without anyone caring.

Some traces of senior year also stayed with me in university. For me, the most important trace is music. I listened to a lot of piano pieces for homework and sleep during senior year, and I still keep the habit of listening to piano music while studying. Hearing those familiar melodies makes me feel as if I’ve returned to those evening self-study sessions where everyone was exhausted, sometimes concentrating and sometimes “relaxing”. Another funny thing: I often associate the Star Wars series I follow with senior year, because I unexpectedly discovered later that although a few of the main themes from Star Wars are a bit noisy, they help me focus. I opened Apple Music to get recommendations, intending to listen to some new pop songs, but instead I found only new piano pieces. How nostalgic. Sometimes, memories need a trigger to surface; for me, the best triggers are actually smell and hearing—less direct than sight, but far more touching.

I now visit SHS frequently, almost once a week, to see Miss CZY and have a meal with her. Every time I go back, I have an illusion that I’m reuniting with my senior-year self, just like those scenes in movies where you turn your head and see your younger self. I’m very grateful to Miss CZY for accompanying me during senior year, which prevented my life from being limited to a dull routine and brought me more vitality. Now, I want to spend her senior year with her. To be honest, I’m not sure if visiting her so often will affect her studies, but I truly hope she does well—not just academically. I hope she doesn’t suffer too much in senior year, that she stays happy and adorable, and that we can eventually end up together.
