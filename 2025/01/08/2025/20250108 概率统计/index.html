概率统计整理

¶第一章 随机事件和概率

¶1.1 随机事件及其运算

  1. 区分概念：样本点、样本空间
  2. 运算规律：
    1. $交换律：A\cup B=B\cup A,AB=BA$
    2. $结合律：(A\cup B)\cup C=A\cup(B\cup C),(AB)C=A(BC)$
    3. $分配律：A\cup(BC)=(A\cup B)(A\cup C),A(B\cup C)=(AB)\cup (AC)$
    4. $对偶律(De Morgan)：\overline{A\cup B}=\bar A\bar B,\overline{AB}=\bar A\cup\bar B$

¶1.2 随机事件的概率

  1. 概率三公理：
    1. $\forall A, P(A)\geq0$
    2. $对于必然事件\Omega, P(\Omega)=1$
    3. $对于两两互不相容的事件A_1,A_2,\cdots,A_n,\cdots,即A_iA_j=\empty,i\neq j,i,i=1,2,\cdots,有P(\bigcup\limits_{i=1}^{+\infty})=\sum\limits_{i=1}^{+\infty}P(A_i)$
  2. 概率性质：
    1. $P(\empty)=0$
    2. $设A_1,A_2,\cdots,A_n满足A_iA_j=\empty,i\neq j,i,j=1,2,\cdots,n,则有P(\bigcup\limits_{i=1}^nA_i)=\sum\limits_{i=1}^nP(A_i)$
    3. $P(\bar A)=1-P(A)$
    4. $A\subset B\Rightarrow P(B-A)=P(B)-P(A),P(B)\geq P(A)$
    5. $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(AB)$

¶1.3 条件概率

  1. $P(B|A)=\dfrac{P(AB)}{P(A)}$
  2. $若B_1, B_2,\cdots,B_n为样本空间\Omega的一个划分，且P(B_i)>0(i = 1,2,\cdots,n)则对任一事件A，有P(A)=\sum\limits_{i=1}^nP(B_i)P(A|B_i)$
  3. $Bayes公式：若B_1, B_2,\cdots,B_n为样本空间\Omega的一个划分，且P(B_i)>0(i = 1,2,\cdots,n)则对任一事件A，有P(B_i|A)=\dfrac{P(AB_i)}{P(A)}=\dfrac{P(B_i)P(A|B_i)}{\sum\limits_{i=1}^nP(B_i)P(A|B_i)}$

¶1.4 随机事件的独立性

  1. $P(AB)=P(A)P(B)则A，B独立$

  2. $P(A_1A_2)=P(A_1)P(A_2)---(1)$

    $P(A_2A_3)=P(A_2)P(A_3)---(2)$

    $P(A_3A_1)=P(A_3)P(A_1)---(3)$

    $P(A_2A_2A_3)=P(A_1)P(A_2)P(A_3)---(4)$

    $(1)(2)(3)成立则A_1,A_2,A_3两两独立，(1)(2)(3)(4)都成立则A_1,A_2,A_3相互独立$

¶第二章 随机变量及其分布

¶2.1 随机变量及其分布函数

  1. $概率分布函数F(x)=P(X\leq x)$

¶2.2 离散随机变量及其分布律

$P(X=x_k)=p_k,k=1,2\cdots称为X的分布律，有性质：$

  1. $p_k\geq0,k=1,2,\cdots,$
  2. $\sum\limits p_k=1$

¶1. 0-1 分布

X 1 0
P p 1-p

$P(X=k)=p^k(1-p)^{1-k},k=0,1$

¶2. 二项分布 X~B(n,p)

  1. 可独立重复进行n次
  2. 每次实验结果只有两个

称n重Bernoulli试验，Bernoulli概型

$P(X=k)=C_n^kp^k(1-p)^{n-k},k=0,1,\cdots,n$

¶3. 泊松分布 X~P(λ)

$Poisson定理：\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}np_n=\lambda>0，则\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}C_n^kp_n^k(1-p_n)^{n-k}=e^{-\lambda}\dfrac{\lambda^k}{k!}$

$P(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\dfrac{\lambda^k}{k!}$

¶2.3 连续型随机变量及其概率密度

$概率分布函数F(x)-=\int\limits_{-\infty}^xf(t)dt,-\infty<x<\infty,其中f(x)是概率密度函数$

$性质$

  1. $f(x)\geq0$
  2. $\int\limits_{-\infty}^\infty f(x)dx=F(+\infty)=1$
  3. $f(x)=F'(x)$
  4. $P(x_0<X\leq x_0+\Delta x)\approx f(x_0)\Delta x$
  5. $P(x=a)=0$

¶1. 均匀分布 X～U(a,b)

$f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{b-a},&a<x<b\0,&其他\end{cases}$

$F(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x<a,\\dfrac{x-a}{b-a},&a\leq x<b,\1,&x\geq b.\end{cases}$

¶2. 指数分布 X~E(λ)

$f(x)=\begin{cases}\lambda e^{-\lambda x},&x>0,\0,&x\leq 0,\end{cases}$

$F(x)=\begin{cases}1-e^{-\lambda x},&x>0,\0,&x\leq0\end{cases}$

¶3. 正态分布 X～N($\mu,\sigma^2$)

$\phi(x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}},-\infty<x<+\infty$

$\Phi(x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int\limits_{-\infty}^xe^{-\frac{t^2}2}dt,-\infty<x<+\infty$

$\phi(x)=\dfrac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}，-\infty<x<+\infty$

$\Phi(x)=\dfrac 1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int\limits_{-\infty}^{x}e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}},-\infty<x\infty$

¶2.4 随机变量函数的分布

$已知X的概率密度函数为f_X(x)，设y=g(x),Y=g(X)的分布函数为f_X(g^{-1}(y))\cdot|\dfrac{dx}{dy}|$

¶第三章 多维随机变量及其分布

¶3.1 二维随机变量及其分布

  1. $F(x,y)=P({X\leq x}\cap{Y\leq y})=P(X\leq x,Y\leq y)$

  2. 性质：

    1. $0\leq F(x,y)\leq 1,F(-\infty,y) = 0,F(x,-\infty) = 0,F(-\infty,-\infty)=0,F(+\infty,+\infty)=1$
    2. $以x或y为主元，都是单调不减函数$
    3. $以x或y为主元，都是右连续函数$
    4. $\forall a<b,c<d,F(b,d)-F(a,d)-F(b,c)+F(a,c)=P(a<x\leq b,c<Y\leq d)\geq 0$

  3. $边缘分布函数F_X(x)=F(x,+\infty),F_Y(y)=F(+\infty,y)$

¶二维离散型随机变量

性质：

  1. $p_{ij}\geq 0$
  2. $\sum\limits_i\sum\limits_j p_{ij}=1$

¶二维连续型随机变量

性质：

  1. $f(x,y)\geq 0$
  2. $\int\limits_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int\limits_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x,y)dxdy=1$
  3. $P((X,Y)\in D)=\iint\limits_D f(x,y)dxdy = 1$
  4. $在f(x,y)的连续点处，有\dfrac{\partial^2F(x,y)}{\partial x\partial y}=f(x,y)$
¶二维均匀分布

$f(x,y)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{G的面积}，&(x,y)\in G\0,&其他\end{cases}$

¶二维正态分布 $(X,Y)\sim N(\mu_1,\sigma_1^2;\mu_2,\sigma_2^2;\rho)$

$f(x,y)=\dfrac1{2\pi\sigma_1\sigma_2\sqrt{1-\rho^2}}exp\bigg{-\dfrac1{2(1-p^2)}\Big [\dfrac{(x-\mu_1)^2}{\sigma_1^2}-2\rho\dfrac{(x-\mu_1)(y-\mu_2)}{\sigma_1\sigma_2}+\dfrac{(y-\mu_2)^2}{\sigma_2^2}\Big]\bigg}$

¶3.2 二维随机变量的条件分布

  1. $P(X=x_i|Y=y_j)=\dfrac{P(X=x_i,Y=y_j)}{P(Y=y_j)}=\dfrac{p_{ij}}{P_{\cdot j}}$

  2. $P(X=x_i)=\sum\limits_j P(Y=y_j)P(X=x_i|Y=y_j)$

  3. $f_{X|Y}(x|y)=\dfrac{f(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}$

    $F_{X|Y}(x|y)=\int\limits_{-\infty}^x\dfrac{f(u,y)}{f_Y(y)}du$

¶3.3 随机变量的独立性

  1. $X与Y相互独立\Leftrightarrow F(x,y)=F_X(x)F_y(y);f_X(x)f_Y(y)$
  2. $设(X,Y)为二维离散型随机变量，联合分布律为P(X=x_i,Y=y_j)=p_{ij},则X，Y相互独立的充要条件为：P(X=x_i,Y=y_j)=P(X=x_i)P(Y=y_j),若连续型，则f(x,y)=f_X(x)f_Y(y)$

¶第四章 随机变量的数字特征

¶4.1 数学期望

  1. $离散：E(X)=\sum\limits_{k=1}^{+\infty}x_kp_k$

    $连续：E(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xf(x)$

  2. $数学期望的性质$

    1. $数学期望存在\Leftrightarrow E|X|<+\infty$
    2. $X\leq Y,则E(X)\leq E(Y)$
    3. $E(C)=0$
    4. $E(CX)=CE(X)$
    5. $E(X+Y)=E(X)+E(Y)$
    6. $若X，Y独立，则E(XY)=E(X)\cdot E(Y)$

¶4.2 方差

  1. $D(X)=E{[X-E(X)]^2}=E(X^2)-[E(X)]^2$

  2. $方差的性质：$

    1. $方差存在\Leftrightarrow E(X^2)<+\infty$
    2. $C为常数\Rightarrow D(C)=0$
    3. $C为常数，则D(CX)=C^2D(X)$
    4. $X和Y独立且方差都存在，则D(X+Y)=D(X)+D(Y)$
    5. $D(X)\leq E[(X-C)^2]$
    6. $D(X)=0\Leftrightarrow \exists C,s.t.P(X=C)=1, C=E(X)$

¶第五章 大数定律和中心极限定理

¶5.1 预备知识

  1. $Chebyshev不等式：P(|X-\mu|\geq \epsilon)\leq \dfrac{\sigma^2}{\epsilon^2},P(|X-\mu|<\epsilon)>1-\dfrac{\sigma^2}{\epsilon^2}$

¶5.2 大数定律

  1. $Bernoulli大数定律：\lim\limits_{n\to+\infty}P(|\dfrac{n_A}n-p|\geq\epsilon)=0,\lim\limits_{n\to+\infty}P(|\dfrac{n_A}n-p|<\epsilon)=1$
  2. $Chebyshev大数定律：\lim\limits_{n\to+\infty}P(|\dfrac1n\sum\limits_{k=1}^n X_k-\dfrac1n\sum\limits_{k=1}^n\mu_k|<\epsilon)=1$
  3. $Khintchine大数定律：\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}P(|\dfrac1n\sum\limits_{k=1}^nX_k-\mu|\geq\epsilon)=0,\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}P(|\dfrac1n\sum\limits_{k=1}^nX_k-\mu|<\epsilon)=1$

¶5.3 中心极限定理

  1. $独立同分布的中心极限定理：设X_1,X_2,\cdots,X_n,\cdots为独立同分布的随机变量序列，\forall x,\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}P(\dfrac{\sum\limits_{k=1}^nX_k-n\mu}{\sqrt n\sigma}\leq x)=\Phi(x)$
  2. $De moivre-Laplace中心极限定理：设随机变量Y_n\sim B(n,p),n\to\infty, Y_n\sim N(np, np(1-p))$

¶6.1 数理统计的基本概念

  1. $\bar X=\dfrac1n \sum\limits_{i=1}^n X_i$
  2. $S^2=\dfrac1{n-1}\sum\limits_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X)^2$

  4. $(CM)k=\dfrac 1n\sum\limits{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X)^k(k=1,2,\cdots)$

¶6.2 几个常用统计量的分布

  1. $正态分布 X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$

  2. $\chi^2分布：$

    $\chi^2=\sum\limits_{i=1}^n X_i^2\sim\chi^2(n)$

    $E(\chi^2)=n,D(\chi^2)=2n$

    $若两者独立，则X_1+X_2\sim \chi^2(n_1+n_2)$

    $n很大时,近似符合正态分布N(n,2n)$

  3. $t分布：$

    $X\sim N(0,1),Y\sim \chi^2(n),T=\dfrac{X}{\sqrt{Y/n}}\sim t(n)$

    $n充分大(>45)时，近似服从标准正态分布$

    $t_{1-\alpha}(n)=-t_\alpha(n)$

  4. $F分布：$

    $U\sim\chi^2(m),V\sim\chi^2(n)，且U,V独立，F=\dfrac{U/m}{V/n}～F(m,n)$

    $F\sim F(m,n)\Rightarrow \dfrac1F\sim F(n,m)$

    $F_{1-\alpha}(m,n)=\dfrac1{F_\alpha(n,m)}$

¶6.3 正态分布的抽样分布

  1. 单个正态总体的抽样分布
    1. $\bar X\sim N(\mu,\dfrac{\sigma^2}{n})$
    2. $\dfrac{(n-1)S^2}{\sigma^2}=\sum\limits_{i=1}^n{\dfrac{X_i-\bar X}{\sigma}}\sim\chi^2(n-1)$
    3. $\bar X与\dfrac{(n-1)S^2}{\sigma^2}独立$
    4. $\cfrac{\bar X-\mu}{S/\sqrt n}\sim t(n-1)$
  2. 两个正态总体的抽样分布
    1. $\dfrac{S_1^2/\sigma_1^2}{S_2^2/\sigma_2^2}\sim F(n-1,m-1)$
    2. $\sigma_1=\sigma_2=\sigma时,\dfrac{(\bar X-\bar Y)-(\mu_1-\mu_2)}{\sqrt{\dfrac1n+\dfrac1m}\sqrt{\dfrac{(n-1)S_1^2+(m-1)S_2^2}{n+m-2}}}$

¶2.4 Distribution of Functions of Random Variables

Given the probability density function of X as f_X(x), let y = g(x). The distribution function of Y = g(X) is f_X(g⁻¹(y)) · |dx/dy|.

¶Chapter 3: Multidimensional Random Variables and Their Distributions

¶3.1 Two-Dimensional Random Variables and Their Distributions

  1. F(x,y) = P({X ≤ x} ∩ {Y ≤ y}) = P(X ≤ x, Y ≤ y)
  2. Properties:
    • 0 ≤ F(x,y) ≤ 1, F(-∞, y) = 0, F(x, -∞) = 0, F(-∞, -∞) = 0, F(+∞, +∞) = 1
    • It is a non-decreasing function when taking x or y as the independent variable.
    • It is a right-continuous function when taking x or y as the independent variable.
    • ∀a < b, c < d, F(b,d) - F(a,d) - F(b,c) + F(a,c) = P(a < X ≤ b, c < Y ≤ d) ≥ 0
  3. Marginal distribution functions: F_X(x) = F(x, +∞), F_Y(y) = F(+, y)

¶Two-Dimensional Discrete Random Variables

Properties:

  • p_ij ≥ 0
  • ∑ᵢ∑ⱼ p_ij = 1

¶Two-Dimensional Continuous Random Variables

Properties:

  • f(x,y) ≥ 0
  • ∫_{-∞}^{∞}∫_{-∞}^{∞} f(x,y)dxdy = 1
  • P((X,Y) ∈ D) = ∬_D f(x,y)dxdy = 1
  • At points where f(x,y) is continuous, ∂²F(x,y)/∂x∂y = f(x,y)

¶Two-Dimensional Normal Distribution (X,Y) ~ N(μ₁, σ₁²; μ₂, σ₂²; ρ)

f(x,y) = \dfrac{1}{2πσ₁σ₂\sqrt{1-ρ²}} exp\left{ -\dfrac{1}{2(1-ρ²)} \left[ \dfrac{(x-μ₁)²}{σ₁²} - 2ρ\dfrac{(x-μ₁)(y-μ₂)}{σ₁σ₂} + \dfrac{(y-μ₂)²}{σ₂²} \right] \right}

¶3.2 Conditional Distributions of Two-Dimensional Random Variables

  1. P(X = xᵢ | Y = yⱼ) = \dfrac{P(X = xᵢ, Y = yⱼ)}{P(Y = yⱼ)} = \dfrac{p_ij}{P_{·j}}

  2. P(X = xᵢ) = ∑ⱼ P(Y = yⱼ) P(X = xᵢ | Y = yⱼ)

  3. f_{X|Y}(x|y) = \dfrac{f(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}

    F_{X|Y}(x|y) = ∫_{-∞}^x \dfrac{f(u,y)}{f_Y(y)}du

¶3.3 Independence of Random Variables

  1. X and Y are independent ⇔ F(x,y) = F_X(x)F_Y(y); f_X(x)f_Y(y)
  2. If (X,Y) is a two-dimensional discrete random variable with joint probability mass function P(X = xᵢ, Y = yⱼ) = p_ij, then X and Y are independent if and only if: P(X = xᵢ, Y = yⱼ) = P(X = xᵢ)P(Y = yⱼ). For continuous random variables, this condition is f(x,y) = f_X(x)f_Y(y).

¶Chapter 4: Numerical Characteristics of Random Variables

¶4.1 Mathematical Expectation

  1. Discrete case: E(X) = ∑_{k=1}^{+∞} x_kp_k

    Continuous case: E(x) = ∫_{-∞}^{+∞} xf(x)dx

  2. Properties of mathematical expectation:

    • The mathematical expectation exists ⇔ E|X| < +∞
    • If X ≤ Y, then E(X) ≤ E(Y)
    • E(C) = C
    • E(CX) = CE(X)
    • E(X+Y) = E(X) + E(Y)
    • If X and Y are independent, then E(XY) = E(X) · E(Y)

¶4.2 Variance

  1. D(X) = E{[X - E(X)]²} = E(X²) - [E(X)]²
  2. Properties of variance:
    • The variance exists ⇔ E(X²) < +∞
    • For a constant C, D(C) = 0
    • For a constant C, D(CX) = C²D(X)
    • If X and Y are independent and their variances exist, then D(X+Y) = D(X) + D(Y)
    • D(X) ≤ E[(X-C)²]
    • D(X) = 0 ⇔ ∃C such that P(X = C) = 1, and C = E(X)

¶4.4 Covariance and Correlation Coefficient

  1. cov(X,Y) = E(XY) - E(X)E(Y)
  2. ρ_{XY} = \dfrac{cov(X,Y)}{\sqrt{D(X)}\sqrt{D(Y)}}
  3. D(X ± Y) = D(X) + D(Y) ± 2cov(X,Y)
  4. Properties of covariance:
    • cov(X,Y) = cov(Y,X)
    • cov(aX,bY) = abcov(X,Y)
    • cov(X+Y,Z) = cov(X,Z) + cov(Y,Z)
    • |cov(X,Y)| ≤ \sqrt{D(X)}\sqrt{D(Y)}
  5. Properties of correlation coefficient:
    • |ρ_{XY}| ≤ 1
  6. Equivalent statements for uncorrelatedness:
    • X and Y are uncorrelated
    • ρ_{XY} = 0
    • cov(X,Y) = 0
    • E(XY) = E(X)E(Y)
    • D(X ± Y) = D(X) + D(Y)
    • D(X+Y) = D(X-Y)

¶4.5 Higher-Order Moments of Random Variables

  • k-th order raw moment: E(|X|^k)
  • k-th order central moment: E{[X - E(X)]ᵏ}
  • k+l-th order mixed raw moment: E(|X|^k|Y|^l)
  • k+l-th order mixed central moment: E{[X - E(X)]ᵏ[Y - E(Y)]ᵉ}

¶Chapter 5: Law of Large Numbers and Central Limit Theorem

¶5.1 Preliminary Knowledge

  1. Chebyshev's inequality: P(|X-μ| ≥ ε) ≤ \dfrac{σ²}{ε²}, P(|X-μ| < ε) > 1 - \dfrac{σ²}{ε²}

¶5.2 Law of Large Numbers

  1. Bernoulli's law of large numbers: \lim_{n→+∞} P(|\dfrac{n_A}{n} - p| ≥ ε) = 0, \lim_{n→+∞} P(|\dfrac{n_A}{n} - p| < ε) = 1
  2. Chebyshev's law of large numbers: \lim_{n→+∞} P(|\dfrac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^n X_k - \dfrac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^n μ_k| < ε) = 1
  3. Khintchine's law of large numbers: \lim_{n→∞} P(|\dfrac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^n X_k - μ| ≥ ε) = 0, \lim_{n→∞} P(|\dfrac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^n X_k - μ| < ε) = 1

¶5.3 Central Limit Theorem

  1. Central limit theorem for independent and identically distributed variables: Let X₁, X₂, ⋯, Xₙ, ⋯ be a sequence of independent and identically distributed random variables. ∀x, \lim_{n→∞} P(\dfrac{\sum_{k=1}^n X_k - nμ}{\sqrt{n}σ} ≤ x) = Φ(x)
  2. De Moivre-Laplace central limit theorem: If the random variable Yₙ ~ B(n,p), as n → ∞, Yₙ ~ N(np, np(1-p)).

¶Chapter 6: Preliminary Knowledge of Mathematical Statistics

¶6.1 Basic Concepts of Mathematical Statistics

  1. \bar{X} = \dfrac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n X_i
  2. S² = \dfrac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^n (X_i - \bar{X})²
  3. M_k = \dfrac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n X_i^k (k=1,2,⋯)
  4. (CM)k = \dfrac{1}{n} \sum{i=1}^n (X_i - \bar{X})^k (k=1,2,⋯)

¶6.2 Distributions of Several Common Statistics

  1. Normal distribution: X ~ N(μ, σ²)
  2. χ² distribution:
    • χ² = ∑_{i=1}^n X_i² ~ χ²(n)
    • E(χ²) = n, D(χ²) = 2n
    • If two χ² variables are independent, then X₁ + X₂ ~ χ²(n₁ + n₂)
    • When n is large, it approximately follows a normal distribution N(n, 2n).
  3. t distribution:
    • X ~ N(0,1), Y ~ χ²(n), T = \dfrac{X}{\sqrt{Y/n}} ~ t(n)
    • When n is sufficiently large (>45), it approximately follows a standard normal distribution.
    • t_{1-α}(n) = -t_α(n)
  4. F distribution:
    • U ~ χ²(m), V ~ χ²(n), and U, V are independent, then F = \dfrac{U/m}{V/n} ~ F(m,n)
    • If F ~ F(m,n), then \dfrac{1}{F} ~ F(n,m)
    • F_{1-α}(m,n) = \dfrac{1}{F_α(n,m)}

¶6.3 Sampling Distributions of the Normal Distribution

  1. Sampling distributions for a single normal population:
    • \bar{X} ~ N(μ, \dfrac{σ²}{n})
    • \dfrac{(n-1)S²}{σ²} = ∑_{i=1}^n {\dfrac{X_i - \bar{X}}{σ}} ~ χ²(n-1)
    • \bar{X} and \dfrac{(n-1)S²}{σ²} are independent.
    • \cfrac{\bar{X} - μ}{S/\sqrt{n}} ~ t(n-1)
  2. Sampling distributions for two normal populations:
    • \dfrac{S₁²/σ₁²}{S₂²/σ₂²} ~ F(n-1,m-1)
    • When σ₁ = σ₂ = σ, \dfrac{(\bar{X} - \bar{Y}) - (μ₁ - μ₂)}{\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{n} + \dfrac{1}{m}} \sqrt{\dfrac{(n-1)S₁² + (m-1)S₂²}{n+m-2}}}

¶Chapter 8: Hypothesis Testing

Determine the test statistic and rejection region.

¶8.1 Hypothesis Testing for the Mean of a Single Normal Population

  1. When the variance is known:
    • U = \dfrac{\bar{X} - μ₀}{σ/\sqrt{n}} ~ N(0,1)
  2. When the variance is unknown:
    • T = \dfrac{\bar{X} - μ₀}{S/\sqrt{n}} ~ t(n-1)

¶8.2 Hypothesis Testing for the Variance of a Single Normal Population

  1. When the mean is known:
    • χ² = \dfrac{∑_{i=1}^n (X_i - μ)²}{σ₀²} ~ χ²(n)
  2. When the mean is unknown:
    • χ² = \dfrac{(n-1)S²}{σ₀²} ~ χ²(n-1)

¶8.3 Hypothesis Testing for the Difference in Means of Two Normal Populations

  1. When the variances σ₁², σ₂² are known:
    • \bar{X} - \bar{Y} ~ N(δ, \dfrac{σ₁²}{n} + \dfrac{σ₂²}{m})
  2. When σ₁² = σ₂² = σ² is unknown:
    • T = \dfrac{\bar{X} - \bar{Y} - δ}{S_W\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{n} + \dfrac{1}{m}}} ~ t(n+m-2)
  3. When σ₁², σ₂² are unknown but the sample sizes are equal:
    • \dfrac{\bar{Z} - δ}{S_Z/\sqrt{n}} ~ t(n-1)
