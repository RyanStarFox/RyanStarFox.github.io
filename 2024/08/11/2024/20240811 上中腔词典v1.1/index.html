前言

v1.0

写这一份上中腔词典的原因是一时兴起。其实很难说我这里所说的“上中腔”的具体定义是什么。说是上中腔，但又主要是大师班的同学常用，但别的班也有一批同学会用。说是大师班的同学常用，但又主要是大师班的男生在用，女生们也会用其中的一些表述，但不多。为了简单起见，还是称其为上中腔好了。写这份东西写主要是图一乐，回顾一下以前的一些已经成为典故的趣事，不可当真。用上中腔的表述来说，这份东西其实非常🌭，ydfx。编写这份东西的时候，毛哥和Nababa同学给我提供了很多帮助，他们是我的第一批读者，帮助我扩充了一些词条，也回忆了有意思的故事，其中Nababa同学还帮我写了一些我不是很熟悉的词条。感谢zjh同学，在发布前夕又补充了大量词条。也感谢myj，sty同学，从他们那里我也了解到更多的一些我不熟悉的词条和它们的词源典故。以下的词条的例句来源于我与同学的聊天记录，感谢这些聊天记录！这些必然包含着我的片面性与局限性，欢迎扩充更多词条以及修订词条！

v1.1

修订、增补了一些词条。由于微信显示不出\dagger，\ddagger和\star符号，在微信公众号中改成了粗体、斜体和下划线。也欢迎下载原文档查看。感谢Nababa同学和try同学的批评指正以及提供的资料。
关于到底什么是“上中腔”，try同学认为这是九班腔，又因高三重分班后在大师班内广泛传播，随后又平行班也沾染了一些这种腔调。认为有一定道理，不知大家怎么看。感觉这确实很难定义，但确实有这么个东西。它可以是上中腔、九班腔甚至只是我身边的人的口癖。感觉这份东西还是非常主观啊！嗨不过也是写这好玩儿而已。只是一份🌭词典罢了
估计也就到1.1就差不多了吧。如果这份东西还能再写一个1.2就真的🌭又fx了。

2. 假文言

人称代词+母

  • 【释义】xx的母亲
  • 【变体】你母，我母
  • 【例句】好吧，你母今年贵庚？

表示程度的词+佳$^\star$

  • 【释义】表示对事物的评判，一般不用好、坏，而是用“佳”
  • 【变体】不佳、极佳、最佳、较佳
  • 【例句】哎呀，外面天气确不佳。

何处

  • 【释义】在哪里
  • 【例句】届时具体何处会面，可再研。

亦$^\star$

  • 【释义】也
  • 【例句】但他们亦用苹果。

可/不可$^\star$

  • 【释义】可以
  • 【例句】kb，如果有体育，则不可。

3. 大词小用

部署$^\star$

  • 【释义】筹划，准备，实施
  • 【例句】哦，明早你起床后部署一下收款。
  • 【例句】且室友今天还在部署去日本玩的机票。

会面$^\star$

  • 【释义】见面，碰头
  • 【例句】届时具体何处会面，可再研。

昏聩

  • 【释义】昏了头，做出一些荒谬或者愚蠢的事情
  • 【例句】早上淑芬很昏聩。

拒绝$^\star$

  • 【释义】语气词，表示拒绝，但是语气不正式，有点委婉地表示拒绝，常与微信[抱拳]表情包连用
  • 【例句】拒绝到国权路。

令人心寒

  • 【释义】让人感到寒心，夸张化表述

视奸

  • 【释义】偷偷监视
  • 【词源】双枪小帅对于董行为的描述（经常偷偷在后门监视同学们自修上课是否认真）
  • 【例句】被你视奸。

通过$^\star$

  • 【释义】表示赞同
  • 【近义词】支持
  • 【例句】可以可以，通过，接受了。

污名化

  • 【释义】表示不承认对方对自己的批评，常与拒绝、禁止连用，有时也用于承认自己的批评不妥当
  • 【词源】中国外交部常用语
  • 【例句】hd你说的对，污名化一次。

研究$^\star$

  • 【释义】了解一下
  • 【变体】研究一下，经我研究
  • 【例句】所以研究你有无入坑想法。

支持$^\star$

  • 【释义】表示赞同
  • 【近义词】通过
  • 【例句】狠狠支持了。

    后面由于音近，有呕人变“体操”为“蕊尻”，更加发笑。

无头苍蝇$^\dagger$

“董”相关$^\dagger$

胡椒$^\dagger$

看看你的$^\star$

  • 【释义】看看对方的情况
  • 【例句】[发送了一张图片，是一个量表]看看你的！

口区$^\dagger$

  • 【释义】表示恶心
  • 【词源】“呕”这个字拆开来，来源于孙m同学，rui同学最爱用，后来风靡开来了
  • 【变体】呕（先有的“口区”，再有的“呕”）

蓝奶酪${^\dagger} {^\ddagger}$

  • 【释义】味道恶臭的物品。
  • 【词源】来自高二地理课张yt发现所坐座位有恶臭，经沈ty研究恶臭来源为龚zr的自制酸奶，故用同为恶臭奶制品的蓝奶酪代指。

某巨$^\dagger$

  • 【释义】对厉害的人的尊称
  • 【例词】郭巨

谬误

  • 【释义】表示用轻松的语气对某人的批评，常与完全连用
  • 【例句】我又发现了一些小谬误。

你好$^\star$

  • 【释义】表示接下来说的内容将以一种戏剧化或者夸张化的语气，常与“同学”、“请”连用
  • 【例句】你好，请飞来北京看。

脑控

  • 【释义】嘲讽对方做了一些比较荒谬的事情
  • 【词源】原出处已散逸，或许来源于廖凯原的《轩辕反熵运行体系》，或许来源于网上关于脑控的发笑阴谋论

脑死亡$^\star$

  • 【释义】做了一些荒谬或者略微有点愚蠢、没有逻辑的事情，可以形容自己或者别人，贬义。常与热狗连用。
  • 【词源】来源于《破道计划》（郭xj著）“所有人眼前一黑，全部倒下，他们的大脑承载了本不该承载的东西，全部陷入了脑死亡。”
  • 【变体】nsw、脑死亡一次、nsw吧、脑死、眼前一黑，陷入脑死亡
  • 【例句】我今天早上11:15起床已经nsw！

Odeur$^\dagger$

  • 【释义】匿名者
  • 【词源】网课期间，沈ty同学以Odeur的化名出没于各个腾讯会议会议室

然而

  • 【释义】表示转折
  • 【例句】然而：我可悲。

热狗$^\star$

  • 【释义】表示做事不太聪明又有点可爱发笑，可作为名词、形容词、副词、语气词使用
  • 【词源】高一义卖高sm同学提议可以卖热狗，后虽热狗因卫生原因被否，但是高sm同学读“热狗”的口音流传了下来
  • 【变体】热狗人，🌭，🌭吧，小热狗以及众多表情包的形式
  • 【例句】这也是为什么有小热狗喜欢iPad mini。

请$^\star$

  • 【释义】发语词，表示接下来说的内容将以一种戏剧化或者夸张化的语气，有时用来表达比较轻松语气的请教，常与同学、你好连用
  • 【例句】请你加速！

事实上

  • 【释义】in fact，也不知为何常被拿来发语，语言非常欧化呐
  • 【例句】事实上我后天早上说不定也要从交大出发。

同学$^\star$

  • 【释义】表示接下来说的内容将以一种戏剧化或者夸张化的语气，常与请、你好连用、注意
  • 【例句】同学，你有些发笑。

tsosnymud$^\dagger$

  • 【释义】“操死你妈的”用外国人口音读出来
  • 【词源】来源于章jh同学对“操你妈的”进行加强，并且用更顺口的语气说出来。

完全

  • 【例句】我显然追求最佳显示效果。

智慧

Preface

v1.0

I wrote this Shangzhong dialect dictionary on a whim. It’s actually hard to define exactly what I mean by “Shangzhong dialect.” I call it Shangzhong dialect, but it’s mainly used by classmates in the Master Class, though a bunch of students in other classes use it too. I say it’s mainly used by Master Class students, but it’s mainly the boys in the Master Class who use it; the girls use some of these expressions too, but not many. For simplicity’s sake, let’s just call it Shangzhong dialect. I’m writing this mainly for fun, to look back on some amusing incidents that have already become lore—don’t take it seriously. To put it in Shangzhong dialect: this thing is really 🌭, ydfx. While writing this, Mao-ge and Nababa gave me a lot of help. They were my first readers, helped me expand some entries, and recalled interesting stories; Nababa even helped me write some entries I wasn’t very familiar with. Thanks to zjh, who added a large number of entries right before publication. Also thanks to myj and sty, from whom I learned about more entries I wasn’t familiar with and their etymological lore. The example sentences below come from my chat records with classmates—thanks to these chat records! This inevitably contains my one-sidedness and limitations; additions of more entries and revisions are welcome!

v1.1

Revised and added some entries. Since WeChat can’t display \dagger, \ddagger, and \star symbols, in the WeChat public account they were changed to bold, italics, and underline. You’re also welcome to download the original document to view it. Thanks to Nababa and try for their criticism, corrections, and the materials they provided.
As for what exactly “Shangzhong dialect” is, try thinks it’s Class 9 dialect, which after the senior-year class reshuffling spread widely within the Master Class and then some of this tone also rubbed off on the parallel classes. I think there’s some truth to this—not sure what everyone else thinks. It really is hard to define, but there really is such a thing. It could be Shangzhong dialect, Class 9 dialect, or even just the verbal tics of the people around me. This thing is still very subjective! Oh well, it’s just for fun anyway. It’s just a 🌭 dictionary.
I figure it’ll probably stop at 1.1. If this thing gets a 1.2, that would really be 🌭 and fx again.

3. Big Words Used for Small Things

部署$^\star$ (deploy)

  • 【Definition】plan, prepare, carry out
  • 【Example】Oh, deploy the payment collection after you get up tomorrow morning.
  • 【Example】And my roommate is still deploying plane tickets to Japan today.

会面$^\star$ (meet)

  • 【Definition】meet, get together
  • 【Example】We can discuss again exactly where to meet at that time.

昏聩 (senile/confused)

  • 【Definition】to be muddle-headed and do absurd or stupid things
  • 【Example】Shufen was very muddle-headed this morning.

拒绝$^\star$ (refuse)

  • 【Definition】a particle expressing refusal, but in an informal tone, somewhat euphemistically refusing; often used together with the WeChat [fist salute] emoji
  • 【Example】Refuse to go to Guoquan Road.

令人心寒 (chilling)

  • 【Definition】to make one feel cold at heart; an exaggerated expression

视奸 (creep on)

  • 【Definition】to secretly monitor
  • 【Etymology】Shuangqiang Xiaoshuai’s description of Dong’s behavior (often secretly monitoring classmates from the back door to see whether they study seriously in self-study or class)
  • 【Example】Creeped on by you.

通过$^\star$ (pass)

  • 【Definition】to express approval
  • 【Synonyms】support
  • 【Example】Okay okay, passed, accepted.

污名化 (stigmatize)

  • 【Definition】to express non-recognition of the other party’s criticism of oneself; often used with refuse and prohibit; sometimes also used to admit that one’s own criticism was inappropriate
  • 【Etymology】A common phrase of the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs
  • 【Example】hd you’re right, stigmatized once.

研究$^\star$ (research)

  • 【Definition】to look into
  • 【Variants】research a bit, according to my research
  • 【Example】So research whether you have any intention of getting into it.

支持$^\star$ (support)

  • 【Definition】to express approval
  • 【Synonyms】pass
  • 【Example】Strongly supported.

4. Math-Related

笛卡尔的茅厕$^\dagger$ (Descartes’ outhouse)

  • 【Definition】analytic geometry
  • 【Etymology】Descartes was the founder of analytic geometry. In Guo fj’s pure geometry cup competition “Grand Sima Cup” call to action, it was mentioned: “Descartes’ outhouse attracted many headless flies.”

号角已经吹响$^\dagger$ (the horn has sounded)

  • 【Definition】begin
  • 【Etymology】Also from the “Grand Sima Cup” call to action: “The horn of the Grand Sima Cup has already sounded.”

数学是思维的体操$^\dagger$ (math is gymnastics for the mind)

  • 【Definition】math can train one’s thinking, just as gymnastics can train one’s body
  • 【Etymology】From the public account “Math is Gymnastics for the Mind.” The manager of this public account is a member of the Plane Geometry Bar and once asked Guo fj to help solve plane geometry problems. Later this public account’s name spread through Guo fj in Class ⑨, and because the name was so bizarre it became a meme.
    Later, due to similar pronunciation, some disgusting person changed “gymnastics” to “ruikao,” making it even funnier.
  • 【Variants】math is gymnastics for the ruikao

无头苍蝇$^\dagger$ (headless fly)

  • 【Definition】a person who does brainless things
  • 【Etymology】Also from Guo fj’s pure geometry cup competition “Grand Sima Cup” call to action, which mentioned: “Descartes’ outhouse attracted many headless flies.”

5. Imitating Teachers

像像我和你讲/说 (like-like I tell you)

  • 【Definition】an opening particle, used in a joking tone to notify or advise the other party
  • 【Etymology】Teacher Wu jy sometimes stutters a bit; her own catchphrase is “like I tell you,” but because of the stutter it became “like-like I tell you”

you cross the line$^\dagger$

  • 【Definition】you crossed the line
  • 【Etymology】A flyer assigned by Teacher Li p; one group used superb PS skills to edit out Jie-ge helping classmates buy things and solve housing problems, and after being discovered they were criticized for crossing the line

whip$^\dagger$

  • 【Definition】to whip
  • 【Etymology】To explain the meaning of the word whip, Teacher Li p demonstrated whipping little f in the classroom

6. Others

be$^\star$

  • 【Definition】a particle pronounced “be” in Shanghainese, often used with weisa, can also be written as “beh”
  • 【Etymology】In freshman year, Zhong zj was doing prerequisite-course homework during Sunday evening self-study and shouted in the classroom: “be, what is linear dependence?”

dismiss$^\star$

  • 【Definition】to refuse, abandon, cut off ties; used in a joking tone
  • 【Example】She has already been dismissed by me

保守估计98分$^\dagger$ (conservatively estimate 98 points)

  • 【Definition】to indicate that one is bluffing
  • 【Etymology】After a certain history exam ended, rui shouted loudly: “Conservatively estimate 98 points.”
  • 【Variants】conservatively estimate
  • 【Example】Today I conservatively estimate the fund can earn 500

刺痛 (sting)

  • 【Definition】to indicate that some things negatively affect one’s mood
  • 【Example】For example, bringing up my failure in advanced math is a kind of sting.

“董”-related$^\dagger$

  • 【Definition】a series of words derived from Teacher Dong jq, such as “not Dong pretending to be Dong,” “Dong pretending not to be Dong”
  • 【Etymology】In freshman year Teacher Dong jq was elusive. Sometimes when someone entered the class or peeked from the back door, people suspected it was Teacher Dong, hence “not Dong pretending to be Dong,” and from this a series of words was derived

胡椒$^\dagger$ (pepper)

  • 【Definition】an anonymous person who seems very impressive
  • 【Etymology】After the spring exam, English expert Cao yw asked the teacher in the English group under the anonymous id “Pepper” whether they could stop doing English homework.

基里巴斯$^\dagger$ (Kiribati)

  • 【Definition】time-space confusion, jet lag
  • 【Etymology】Cosmo reviewed Chinese before the math exam and math before the Chinese exam; time-space was confused, as if living in Kiribati straddling the International Date Line

看看你的$^\star$ (let’s see yours)

  • 【Definition】to see the other person’s situation
  • 【Example】[sent an image, a scale] Let’s see yours!

口区$^\dagger$ (mouth district)

  • 【Definition】to express disgust
  • 【Etymology】The character “呕” (vomit) split apart; from Sun m, rui loved using it most, and later it caught on
  • 【Variants】呕 (the “mouth district” came first, then “vomit”)

蓝奶酪${^\dagger} {^\ddagger}$ (blue cheese)

  • 【Definition】an item with a foul smell.
  • 【Etymology】From sophomore-year geography class when Zhang yt discovered the seat he was sitting in had a foul smell; Shen ty researched the source of the foul smell and found it was Gong zr’s homemade yogurt, so blue cheese, also a foul-smelling dairy product, was used to refer to it.

某巨$^\dagger$ (a certain giant)

  • 【Definition】an honorific for an impressive person
  • 【Example words】Guo giant

谬误 (fallacy)

  • 【Definition】to express criticism of someone in a relaxed tone, often used with “completely”
  • 【Example】I’ve discovered some small fallacies again.

你好$^\star$ (hello)

  • 【Definition】indicates that what follows will be in a dramatic or exaggerated tone, often used with “classmate” and “please”
  • 【Example】Hello, please fly to Beijing to see it.

脑控 (brain control)

  • 【Definition】to mock the other party for doing something rather absurd
  • 【Etymology】The original source has been lost; perhaps from Liao Kaiyuan’s “Xuanyuan Anti-Entropy Operating System,” or perhaps from funny conspiracy theories about brain control online

脑死亡$^\star$ (brain death)

  • 【Definition】to have done something absurd or slightly stupid and illogical; can describe oneself or others; derogatory. Often used with hot dog.
  • 【Etymology】From “Plan Breaking the Dao” (by Guo xj): “Everyone’s vision went black, all fell down, their brains bore things they should not have borne, and all sank into brain death.”
  • 【Variants】nsw, brain death once, nsw ba, brain-dead, vision goes black, sink into brain death
  • 【Example】I got up at 11:15 this morning and am already nsw!

Odeur$^\dagger$

  • 【Definition】anonymous person
  • 【Etymology】During online classes, Shen ty appeared under the alias Odeur in various Tencent Meeting rooms

然而 (however)

  • 【Definition】to express a turn
  • 【Example】However: I am pitiful.

热狗$^\star$ (hot dog)

  • 【Definition】indicates doing something not very smart but a bit cute and funny; can be used as a noun, adjective, adverb, or particle
  • 【Etymology】During the freshman-year charity sale, Gao sm suggested selling hot dogs; although hot dogs were later rejected for hygiene reasons, Gao sm’s accent when reading “hot dog” has been passed down
  • 【Variants】hot dog person, 🌭, 🌭 ba, little hot dog, and many meme forms
  • 【Example】This is also why some little hot dogs like the iPad mini.

请$^\star$ (please)

  • 【Definition】an opening particle, indicating that what follows will be in a dramatic or exaggerated tone; sometimes used to ask for advice in a relatively relaxed tone; often used with classmate and hello
  • 【Example】Please speed up!

事实上 (in fact)

  • 【Definition】in fact; for some reason it’s often used as an opening particle; the language is very Europeanized
  • 【Example】In fact I might also have to set off from Jiaotong University the morning after tomorrow.

同学$^\star$ (classmate)

  • 【Definition】indicates that what follows will be in a dramatic or exaggerated tone; often used with please, hello, and attention
  • 【Example】Classmate, you’re a bit funny.

tsosnymud$^\dagger$

  • 【Definition】“fuck your mother” read with a foreign accent
  • 【Etymology】From Zhang jh strengthening “fuck your mother” and saying it in a smoother tone.

完全 (completely)

  • 【Definition】to express exaggerated negation
  • 【Example】Completely wrong!

显然$^\star$ (obviously)

  • 【Definition】to express an exaggerated tone
  • 【Example】I obviously pursue the best display effect.

一次$^\star$ (once)

  • 【Definition】used for self-mockery, often with laugh and hot dog
  • 【Example】🌭 once

智慧 (wisdom)

  • 【Definition】often used for self-praise; its negative form often expresses criticism in a relaxed tone
  • 【Example】I am wise.
  • 【Example】Very unwise!

中情局$^\dagger$ (CIA)

  • 【Definition】to peek at English dictation answers
  • 【Etymology】In freshman year, Zhong zj, as English class representative, was Prometheus among timely rain; he stole answers from the teacher or neighboring classmates, historically known as the CIA.

注意$^\star$ (attention)

  • 【Definition】to remind the other party that they said something wrong or did something absurd; often used with “intelligence”
  • 【Example】Attention! It only takes 1h5min to get to Hongqiao.
