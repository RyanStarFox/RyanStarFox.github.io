高光时刻–还可以吧

新年快乐🎉🎉

这是我第一次在假期里的苹果发布会，说来好笑，交大竟至今仍未开学

我的直观感受此次发布会无甚惊喜，基本都是常规更新，也有几款产品更新得很烂，真的毫无诚意

iPhone 系列

首先，iPhone16这个外观和颜色真的丑啊！！

我其实很早之前就看到iPhone16的爆料渲染图了，看到这个设计的时候真的觉得是丑啊。我可以理解为了支持空间视频的功能，两个摄像头不能对角线放置，要水平/竖直放置，但是这个摄像头模块让人感到非常不协调：为什么要把闪光灯单独拉到摄像模块之外呢？如果用iPhone X那样的设计或者用iPhone 12那样的设计，我还是可以理解的，但是为了标新立异弄成这样，真的是可可又悲悲。不过不要低估苹果的畜牧业，我看了这个设计看了半年，竟然已被驯化，觉得这个外观还可以了，真的是果粉可悲的自适应。

其次，这个颜色真的有点奇怪。黑白是常规的颜色，另外三款颜色我认为不比去年（15系列的颜色真的是太好看了！），也是有点标新立异。其实我认为最大的问题在于摄像模块的颜色比周围深，色差较大，给人奇怪的感觉。三款彩色中，我认为绿色最佳，蓝色平平，粉色好丑。Hertz认为配色像垃圾桶，过于廉价。他这么一说真的回不来了。

比较令我惊讶的是iPhone16竟然也给了摄影键，本在惊叹刀钝了，没想到刀法依旧——压感功能是Pro exclusive的。

16Pro让人有点期待，毕竟A18Pro是第二代3纳米芯片，应该能解决去年A17Pro的发热问题，这才是满血3纳米！不过比较好笑，官方介绍里的对比默认对比的竟然是12Pro，一下让人觉得哑然失笑。

别的都是常规升级吧？我有一点没弄清楚，这次全系都有“融合式摄像头”，在标准版能承担广角和长焦的工作，有人看懂是什么意思嘛？还是说仅仅是另一个噱头？感觉标准版的长焦还是需要依靠裁切+算法。

Apple watch 系列

watch ultra只是多了新配色，无聊，不表。

watch x可以聊聊：官方给出的最大卖点是薄，不过私以为对于手表来说其实厚度的感知没有那么大，续航才是对于使用体验更重要的部分。目前的续航，正常用可以一天有点宽裕，但是如果旅游、记录大量运动，那么连一天的续航都没有，我在旅途中都是开低电量模式的，这会牺牲常亮显示、运动监测等功能。诚然，这一代升级了快充，但是谁会出门带一根watch的线呢？虽然第三方有小巧的充电头，但是那种又只能超级慢充，也不舒服。所以我认为走偏了——包括上半年的iPad pro我也觉得是类似的问题，不过那个变薄还比watch x变薄更有道理一些，上述的一些缺点并没有，而且变薄变轻的确舒适。

不过leo主张，现在的watch是比正常的watch厚一些，变薄是有道理的，体验确实会更好，不过让人觉得不如ultra 1。

比较牛的是熄屏显示可以1Hz刷新率了，所以说可以显示秒针了。以及亮面铝金属外壳看上去好看一些了，我一开始还以为是不锈钢。

另一件好笑的是：官方声称watch x边框变薄了，我认为我和绝大多数用户都想看到其相对于789的模具变薄了多少，结果官网只有和3456se对比，真是此地无银三百两。

AirPods系列

先说说好笑的AirPods Max，所有的升级只有新配色和type-C，芯片没换性能无提升——这不是和AirPods pro 2（type- C）一样，悄咪咪上架就行了嘛？我不要新颜色，能不能给我AirPods Max2

AirPods 4我认为不错，降噪效果能有AirPods pro一代的效果（还是从AirPods Max的介绍里推断出来的）、无线充电（普通+手表充电器）、力度感应器（就是不知道能不能调整音量，要是可以的话就完美了，不过以苹果的尿性估计是没有的）。总之就是最强半入耳！感觉是在音质和舒适性上找到了平衡点（不喜欢骨传导/耳夹式，音质差&漏音)。

综上所述

觉得还可以吧，中规中矩，不拉胯，但因为没什么有意思的。今年吸取了去年的经验，决定倒买倒卖iPhone 16 Pro Max 沙漠色 1Tb款，成本13999，最多亏0元，不知道能赚多少。

Highlight Moment – So-so

Happy New Year 🎉🎉

This is my first Apple event during a holiday. Funny enough, Jiaotong University still hasn’t started classes yet.

My immediate impression of this event is that there were no major surprises; most updates were routine, and a few products had terrible upgrades with zero sincerity.

iPhone Series

First off, the design and colors of the iPhone 16 are really ugly!!

I saw the leaked renders of the iPhone 16 quite a while ago, and I truly thought the design was hideous. I can understand that to support spatial video functionality, the two cameras can’t be placed diagonally and must be arranged horizontally or vertically. But this camera module looks extremely uncoordinated: why is the flash separated from the camera module? If they used a design similar to the iPhone X or iPhone 12, I could accept it. But doing this just for the sake of being different is really sad. However, don’t underestimate Apple’s ability to condition users — after seeing this design for half a year, I’ve been tamed into thinking the look is acceptable. Truly a tragic case of fan loyalty leading to adaptation.

Secondly, the colors are a bit strange. Black and white are standard colors, but I think the other three colors aren’t as nice as last year’s (the colors of the 15 series were really gorgeous!). The biggest issue is that the color of the camera module is darker than the surrounding area, creating a noticeable color difference that looks odd. Among the three colored models, I think green is the best, blue is mediocre, and pink is ugly. Hertz said the color scheme looks like a trash can and feels too cheap. Once he said that, I couldn’t unsee it.

To my surprise, the iPhone 16 also got a camera button. I was amazed at how predictable Apple’s moves are — the haptic feedback feature is still exclusive to Pro models.

The 16 Pro is somewhat promising; after all, the A18 Pro is a second-generation 3nm chip that should resolve the overheating issues of the A17 Pro from last year. This is the true full-power 3nm chip! It’s funny though that the official comparison in the presentation uses the 12 Pro as the benchmark, which is quite amusing.

Are the other upgrades all routine? There’s one thing I don’t understand: all models this time have a “hybrid camera” that can handle wide-angle and telephoto tasks on the standard version. Does anyone know what this means? Or is it just another marketing gimmick? I feel like the telephoto capability on the standard version still relies on cropping and algorithms.

Apple Watch Series

The Watch Ultra just got new colors — boring, so I won’t cover it.

Let’s talk about the Watch X: the main selling point the official team highlights is its thinness. However, in my opinion, the thickness of a smartwatch isn’t that noticeable to users; battery life is a far more important factor for the overall experience. Currently, the battery can last a full day with moderate use, but if you’re traveling or tracking a lot of workouts, it won’t even last a day. I always use low-power mode when traveling, which disables features like the always-on display and workout tracking. True, this generation supports fast charging, but who would carry a Watch charger when going out? Although third-party manufacturers make compact chargers, they only support slow charging, which is inconvenient. So I think Apple took a wrong turn here — I had a similar view of the iPad Pro released earlier this year; at least its thinner design makes more sense, as it doesn’t have the above flaws, and being thinner and lighter is indeed more comfortable.

However, Leo argues that the current Watch is thicker than normal models, so making it thinner is justified and does improve the experience. Still, he thinks it’s not as good as the Ultra 1.

The coolest update is that the always-on display can now refresh at 1Hz, so the second hand can be displayed. Also, the shiny aluminum casing looks nicer; I initially thought it was stainless steel.

Another funny thing: the official team claimed the Watch X has a thinner bezel. I, and most users, want to see how much thinner it is compared to the 7/8/9 models, but the official website only compares it to the 3/4/5/6/SE models. Talk about trying to hide something!

AirPods Series

Let’s start with the hilarious AirPods Max: the only upgrades are new colors and a USB-C port; the chip hasn’t changed and performance remains the same. Why not just quietly release an AirPods Max 2 instead of rebranding the old model with new colors? I don’t want new colors — give me an AirPods Max 2!

I think the AirPods 4 are great: their noise cancellation is comparable to the first-gen AirPods Pro (I inferred this from the AirPods Max description), they support wireless charging (compatible with regular chargers and Watch chargers), and they have force sensors (I just wonder if volume can be adjusted; if so, they’d be perfect, but given Apple’s track record, that’s unlikely). In short, they’re the best semi-in-ear headphones! They strike a great balance between sound quality and comfort (I dislike bone-conduction and ear-hook designs due to poor sound quality and sound leakage).

Conclusion

I’d say it’s so-so — average, not bad, but nothing interesting. Having learned from last year’s experience, I decided to resell my iPhone 16 Pro Max in Desert Titanium with 1TB storage. The cost was 13,999 yuan, and I stand to lose at most 0 yuan. I wonder how much profit I’ll make.
