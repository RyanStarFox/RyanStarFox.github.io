没抢到果子首发的经历

好笑，时隔一年整，没抢到首发

今年吸取了去年的教训，决定抢贵一点的机型，因为愿意加钱买首发的富哥富姐想必也会无脑冲超级顶配吧？所以我和Bill都筹集了资金，决定一人购置两台iPhone 16 pro max 1t机型

但是事情出现了转机：发布会之后，9/13日之前，我先前去闲鱼进行一些市场调研，发现挂出来的基本上都是256g的机型，也就是iPhone 16 pro max的最低配，令人感到疑惑。可能性有二：

  1. 没有人买1t版本，大家都买256g，所以大家都挂256g
  2. 没有人有1t的货，所以说1t非常抢手

最终我和Bill决定，我购置一台256g，一台1t，Bill购置两台512，平摊风险

我特地在抢之前熟悉了一下流程。去年是在Apple store直接购买的。今年由于原账号转了美区，美区支付方式没有微信支付宝和储蓄卡，只有信用卡，而我没有信用卡，因此只得作罢。在淘宝Apple Store官方、京东Apple自营店、微信小程序Apple store中。最终选择了微信小程序Apple store，因其与Apple store体验最相似。

13日20时，准时开抢！

情况如图，Hertz最为fx，竟然进去了不买，只是去逛逛，像是除夕夜去南京路轧闹猛

随后我出状况了，我之前把钱集中到了我的上海银行卡里面，结果发现有限额，而且限额只有5000，我连转都转不出去。最后还是我妈的协助下购置的两台。虽然20:06就登上了，本应07就付款，结果硬生生一台10分一台15分付的款。

与此同时Bill亦出状况了，他给我打电话，我不接。后来发现又是钱的问题，又是颜色的问题，好像也拖得较晚，但比我略早一些。

买是买上了，有没有首发呢？

去年Leo也是碰到银行卡限额的问题，群里东拼西凑借满了钱，记得是拖到20分上下才付款，结果还是首发，所以应该能抢到吧？

第二天早上起来一看：预计送货日期10/14-18，心凉了半截

于是乎，直接退货，现在已经是这样了，，

与Bill交流发现他亦未抢到首发，他最后不知怎么抢了3台，看上去都是第二批

我帮他挂了闲鱼，看看能不能赚到钱吧。。。

Bill补充分析：

真是奇怪没想到最热门的是白色，经典咏流传啊！新颜色不过尔尔。

综上所述，银行卡限额真的是罪大恶极！！

我气急败坏，于昨日前去上海银行解决此问题，打算将日限额提至每日50k，月限额保持每月100k

昨日先前去观看电影《里斯本丸号沉没》，随后就驱自行车前往上海银行嘉定总行

到号之后又等待许久，才得知这张银行卡既没有工资进出，有没有贷款进出，所以5000的限额已经算是很大了。我疑惑、惊讶，感觉银行、款项大、5000元人民币两两都有关系，可三个放在一起就有些好笑。

银行职员为我提出了两条解决方法：

  1. 转入另一张无限额的银行卡。可是我的别的银行卡都各有各的用处，不想混在一起搞不清楚，作罢
  2. 办一张信用卡，bingo！

我一直都想办一张信用卡，因为对于国际支付方便一些，正好趁此机会顺水推舟办一张

网上查找后，发现可以学生零额度申请而且支持国际支付的推荐去上海银行，于是穿过马路前往中国银行嘉定总行

微信扫了一个码，按照指示输入相关信息后，眼看临门一脚，发现界面加载不出

在浏览器中打开、在中国银行app内均不行

遂作罢，估计中秋系统升级，过两天再尝试

My experience of missing the iPhone 16 Pro Max launch sale

How funny — after a whole year, I still missed the launch sale.

This year, I learned from last year's lesson and decided to go for a pricier model. Since rich folks willing to pay extra for the launch version would likely go for the top-tier configuration anyway, Bill and I pooled our money and decided to each buy two iPhone 16 Pro Max 1TB models.

But then a twist happened: after the launch event, before September 13th, I did some market research on Xianyu and found that almost all listings were for the 256GB model, which is the base variant of the iPhone 16 Pro Max. This was quite puzzling. There are two possible explanations:

  1. No one bought the 1TB version, so everyone listed their 256GB models for sale.
  2. No one had 1TB units in stock, making the 1TB model extremely hard to get.

In the end, Bill and I decided that I would buy one 256GB model and one 1TB model, while Bill would buy two 512GB models, to spread out the risk.

I specifically familiarized myself with the purchase process beforehand. Last year, I bought directly from the Apple Store. This year, since my account was switched to the US region, the US region only supports credit cards as payment methods, and I don’t have a credit card, so I had to look elsewhere. I considered the official Apple Store on Taobao, JD.com's Apple flagship store, and the Apple Store mini-program on WeChat. In the end, I chose the Apple Store mini-program on WeChat because its user experience is most similar to the official Apple Store.

At 8 PM on the 13th, the sale started on time!

As shown in the image, Hertz was acting really weird — he got in but didn’t buy anything, just browsed around, like going to Nanjing Road to join the crowd during Chinese New Year’s Eve.

Then I ran into a problem: I had transferred all my money to my Shanghai Bank card, only to find out it had a daily limit of 5,000 yuan, and I couldn’t even transfer more than that. In the end, I relied on my mom’s help to buy the two phones. Although I logged in at 8:06 PM and was supposed to pay at 8:07 PM, I ended up paying for one phone at 8:10 PM and the other at 8:15 PM.

Meanwhile, Bill also ran into issues. He called me but I didn’t answer. Later I found out it was another problem related to payment and color options, which delayed his purchase a bit, but he finished earlier than me.

So we did manage to buy the phones, but did we get them during the launch sale?

Last year, Leo also faced a bank card limit issue. He borrowed money from friends in the group to meet the requirement and paid around 8:20 PM, yet still got the launch version. So maybe we could still qualify?

The next morning, I checked: the estimated delivery date was October 14–18, which was a huge disappointment.

So I simply returned the phones, and now this is the situation.

I talked to Bill and found out he also missed the launch sale. He somehow ended up buying 3 phones, which all turned out to be from the second batch.

I listed them on Xianyu for him to see if we can make a profit...

Bill’s additional analysis:

It’s really strange — I didn’t expect white to be the most popular color; it’s truly a case of “the classic never fades”! The new colors are just meh.

In summary, bank card limits are truly the worst culprit!!

Furious, I went to Shanghai Bank yesterday to resolve this issue and planned to raise my daily limit to 50,000 yuan and keep my monthly limit at 100,000 yuan.

Yesterday, I first watched the movie The Sinking of the Lisbon Maru, then rode my bike to the Jiading headquarters of Shanghai Bank.

After getting a number, I waited a long time before learning that since this bank card had no salary or loan transactions, a 5,000 yuan limit was already quite generous. I was puzzled and surprised — I feel there’s a connection between banks, large amounts of money, and 5,000 RMB, but putting all three together is kind of funny.

The bank staff gave me two solutions:

  1. Transfer money to another unlimited bank card. But my other bank cards all have specific uses, and I don’t want to mix them up and cause confusion, so I declined.
  2. Apply for a credit card — bingo!

I’ve always wanted to get a credit card because it’s more convenient for international payments. This was the perfect opportunity to take the plunge.

After searching online, I found that students can apply for a zero-limit credit card that supports international payments, and Shanghai Bank was recommended. So I crossed the street to the Jiading headquarters of Bank of China.

I scanned a QR code, entered the required information as instructed, and was just one step away from success when the page failed to load.

It didn’t work either when opened in a browser or through the Bank of China app.

So I gave up and figured the system was undergoing an upgrade for Mid-Autumn Festival, and I’d try again in a few days.

Sigh, my plan to buy an iPhone 16 Pro Max ended in failure — everything went wrong and I fell short.
