对抗推荐，做回自己——反视频推荐脚本「AntiRecommend」

我一直很警惕自己沉迷于某一样东西不能自拔，感觉失去了对于自己的掌控感。

现在其实最容易让人沉迷的就是短视频了，但是我反而不沉迷短视频，一是一开始就保持距离；二是大多短视频都很俗，在声音和画面上都太满了，实在无法忍受。短视频从未成为我沉迷的原因。

长视频反而更让我沉迷。长视频本身就具有迷惑性，它看上去显得更加让人静下心来，也更有用，能获得知识。但是实际上刷的时候开两倍速，无止境地选择下一个推荐的视频——这个逻辑和短视频是一样的。

之前也一直用别的方法限制自己的视频时长。最开始是关闭移动端主页的个性化推荐，随后是删去移动端，只使用桌面端，再之后是桌面端把主页重定向到搜索页。这隐藏了很多数据流，这样以来我几乎只看我关注的博主的视频了——但是还有最后的漏洞：播放器右侧、视频播放临近结束和结束后的推荐与自动连播。

于是乎用vibe coding了AntiRecommend，用于隐藏youtube和bilibili所有的推荐视频，顺便隐藏了一些广告。好用！推荐用！

Against Recommendations, Be Yourself — AntiRecommend, a Script to Fight Video Recommendations

I have always been wary of getting addicted to something and losing my sense of control.

Short videos are usually the easiest trap — but I am not addicted to them. First, I kept my distance from the start. Second, most short videos feel too loud and cluttered in both sound and visuals. Short video never became my addiction.

Long-form video is more seductive. It looks calmer and more useful, as if you are learning something. But watching at 2× speed and endlessly picking the next recommended video follows the same logic as short video.

I have tried many ways to limit my video time: turning off personalized recommendations on mobile, deleting the mobile apps and using only desktop, then redirecting the desktop homepage to search. That hid a lot of the feed, so I mostly watched creators I already follow — but one hole remained: recommendations and autoplay on the right side of the player, near the end of a video, and after it ends.

So I vibe-coded AntiRecommend to hide recommendation videos on YouTube and Bilibili, and some ads along the way. It works well. Recommended!
